# SpeechLab resources

Further reading on speech recognition and transcription.

- [Introducing Whisper — OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/whisper/) — An overview of Whisper's training approach, multilingual capabilities and robustness.
- [Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision — OpenAI (PDF)](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/whisper.pdf) — The Whisper research paper, covering model design, benchmarks and transcription evaluation.
- [Enhancing Whisper transcriptions: pre- & post-processing techniques — OpenAI Cookbook](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/whisper_processing_guide) — An API-based walkthrough of audio preparation and transcript cleanup techniques.
