# Juniper footbridge closure

The Juniper footbridge closes for deck repairs on 6 May 2031 and reopens on 19 May 2031. Works order JF-602 names contractor Moss & Coil. The official pedestrian diversion follows Lantern Lane. Bicycles must be walked along the diversion. The adjacent road bridge stays open. Contact the ward office for step-free access information; no emergency phone number is published here.
