# Brindle Reservoir inspection

The Brindle Reservoir drinking-water inspection starts on 14 July 2031. Rowan Dace leads project BR-713 using the launch Finch. Samples are taken every Thursday at the intake tower and stored in clear polymer bottles. The destination is Stillbank Laboratory. This inland reservoir programme is independent of the coastal Brindle Harbour salt-intrusion survey. Its access authorisation is RI-204.
