# Brindle Harbour survey plan

The Brindle Harbour water survey begins on 14 June 2031 aboard the vessel Lark. Mira Vale coordinates the survey. The project identifier is BH-731. The survey studies salt intrusion near the old seawall, rather than testing drinking water. The crew records tide height before each collection. North Pier is the meeting point for the crew; the pier office does not process samples. Weather postponements are recorded in the field log.
