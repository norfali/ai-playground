# Lantern Lane ferry trial

During the Juniper footbridge repairs, the Lantern ferry trial carries walkers between the east quay and the market steps. It operates from 07:00 to 10:00 on weekdays only. The boat is named Finch and its skipper is Tessa Morn. Trial reference LF-206 covers the ferry timetable. The ferry supplements the signed Lantern Lane walking diversion; it does not replace it.
