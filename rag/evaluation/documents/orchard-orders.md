# Orchard order service

Orchard is the name of a bakery order service. Its release OR-9.3 went live on 3 March 2032, approved by Niko Quill. Order identifiers begin with OTR. The service retries failed receipts after ninety seconds. The support desk is called Gateway. No radio equipment or farm sensors belong to this service. Paper receipts use green ink during the spring promotion.
