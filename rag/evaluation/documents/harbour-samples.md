# Brindle Harbour sample protocol

For project BH-731, water samples are collected at North Pier every Tuesday. Use amber glass bottles and send them to the Saltglass Laboratory. Deliver samples within six hours of collection. The receiving scientist is Orin Pell. Labels must include the project identifier and tide height. This protocol applies to the Brindle Harbour water survey, not the reservoir inspection. No cost estimate is included.
