# Lark Theatre reopening

Lark Theatre reopens on 14 June 2031 with the play North Pier. Mira Vale designed the stage lighting; director Senna Orr coordinates the production. Amber glass bottles are stage props. The theatre booking identifier is LT-731. Visitors enter through the east foyer. The performance concerns an imaginary harbour expedition, but this programme is not a water survey or a laboratory protocol.
