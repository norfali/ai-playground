# Cinder Archive map storage

At the Cinder Archive West Annex, original maps must be stored at 18 degrees Celsius and 45 percent relative humidity. Blue crates are opened only after a twelve-hour acclimatisation period. Conservator Pavo Reed checks the sensors each morning. These conditions apply to paper maps, not photographic negatives. Consult record CA-208 for the relocation schedule; this storage instruction does not repeat the move date.
