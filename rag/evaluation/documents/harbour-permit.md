# Brindle Harbour access permit

Permit BP-418 authorises the Brindle Harbour survey crew to enter North Pier from 06:00 until 11:00. The permit expires on 30 September 2031. The harbour authority contact is Tessa Morn. This document covers pier access only; it does not set the survey start date or the laboratory delivery deadline. The vessel Lark may tie up at berth seven while samples are loaded.
